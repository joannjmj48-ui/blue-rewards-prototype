Danny

The price is held

New mechanic · certainty earned by waiting

The open question in this project is how much forward certainty a Goal can carry when creating one reserves nothing. This is an answer rather than a caveat. Certainty becomes a thing the member earns by holding the Goal, which rewards patience and is the reverse of every urgency mechanic in loyalty. The cost is real: BMO carries the pricing risk, so a live version would need a cap.
